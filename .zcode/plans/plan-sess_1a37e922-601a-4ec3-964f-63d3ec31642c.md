## Tujuan
Wizard `/booking` stage 2 (Rooms & Passengers): untuk trip **BUKAN cruise** (`is_a_cruise_trip=0`), tukar terminologi "Cabin"→"Rooming", auto-pilih jenis bilik jika hanya 1 pilihan, dan guna kategori harga berasaskan **UMUR** (Adult / Children / Infant) bukan **SLOT** (Main Guest / Extra Bed / Infant). Trip cruise kekal model slot sedia ada tanpa perubahan.

## Keputusan reka bentuk (disahkan)
- **Pax_type**: field dah jadi `Data` (free text) — tiada ubah doctype. Non-cruise simpan pax_type `"Adult"/"Children"/"Infant"`; cruise kekal `"Main Guest"/"Extra Bed"/"Infant"`.
- **Solo adult**: flat `price_adult × count` (tiada single supplement).
- **Reuse field payload**: `{room_category, main_guests, extra_beds, infants}` tetap — `main_guests`→Adult, `extra_beds`→Children. Tiada field baharu, tiada migrasi Booking doctype. `booked_pax` sum (`main_guests+extra_beds+infants`) tetap betul.
- **Model kapasiti non-cruise**: adult 1..max_capacity; children 0..(max_capacity−adult) **tanpa** syarat "adult penuh dulu"; infant perlukan ≥1 adult, 0..(max_capacity−adult−children); total ≤ max_capacity. (Cermin cruise: infants kira dalam capacity, sama seperti sedia ada.)
- **Label umur**: Adult (12 tahun ke atas), Children (2–11 tahun), Infant (6–23 bulan).
- **Sumber flag cruise**: `data.trip.is_a_cruise_trip` (sudah dipulangkan `get_booking_details`) → simpan ke `state.is_cruise_trip`. Backend derive dari `Booking.is_a_cruise_trip` (fetch_from trip_date).
