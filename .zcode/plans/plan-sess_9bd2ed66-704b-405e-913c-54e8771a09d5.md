# Pelan: Kemas Kini Sidebar "Travel Booking Management" & Workspace "Travel Booking"

## Context

Navigasi Desk app travel_booking ketinggalan berbanding keadaan semasa app: ada pepijat (link "Booking Wizard" → `/booking` sedangkan laman sebenar `/booknow`; Trip Addon/Trip Addon Package berulang di hujung SUMBER & KREW), 8+ DocType penting tiada link (Cruise Cabin, Booking Amendment, Travel Price Rule, Travel B2B Partner, dll.), halaman Desk wujud tapi tidak dilink, dan workspace ada rujukan tergantung (4 number card + 1 chart + 4 shortcut dalam `content` tetapi array kosong; kad "Dashboard Pages" kosong; kad "Portal Links" tiada Card Break). Kemas kini ini melengkapkan struktur sedia ada (keputusan pengguna: lengkapkan, bukan reka semula) + tambahan 3 skop diluluskan (Number Card + hook sync, buang fail legacy, selaraskan hooks.py pages).

Fakta penting (disahkan): fork Frappe ini menyegerakkan `Sidebar`/`Workspace`/`Dock`/`Page` dari fail app semasa `bench migrate` (`frappe/model/sync.py` — IMPORTABLE_DOCTYPES); `number_card`/`dashboard_chart` TIDAK disegerakkan secara lalai tetapi fork menyokong hook `importable_doctypes` (sync.py:182). `Workspace Link` hanya menyokong link_type `DocType/Page/Report` (tiada URL). Folder page `booking_hub` & `dashboard_reports` tidak wujud di disk walaupun didaftarkan dalam hooks.py.

## Perubahan

### 1. Sidebar — `travel_booking/travel_booking/module_travel_booking/sidebar/travel_booking_management/travel_booking_management.json`
- **Baiki**: URL "Booking Wizard" `/booking` → `/booknow` (www/ tiada booking.html; yang wujud booknow.py/.html).
- **Buang duplikasi**: 2 item terakhir seksyen SUMBER & KREW (Trip Addon, Trip Addon Package — baris ~831–862; sudah ada di TRIP & PRODUK).
- **Tambah link DocType baharu** (ikut bentuk item sedia ada: icon "arrow-big-right", child 1, collapsible 1, open_in_new_tab 1, type Link):
  - TRIP & PRODUK: **Cruise Cabin** (selepas Cruise Schedule)
  - PENERBANGAN: **Flight Schedule** (selepas Flight)
  - TEMPAHAN: **Booking Amendment** (selepas Booking Reservation), **Booking Room Alias** (selepas Rooming (Cruise))
  - VOUCHER & PROMOSI: **Travel Price Rule** (selepas Voucher Usage)
  - SUMBER & KREW: **Crew Allocation** (selepas Crew Slot)
- **Seksyen baharu "RAKANAN B2B & LEAD"** (Section Break, icon "handshake", indent 1, keep_closed 1, show_arrow 1 — ikut pola seksyen lain; letak selepas VOUCHER & PROMOSI): **Travel B2B Partner**, **Travel Inquiry**.
- **HALAMAN DESK**: tambah **Ongoing Today** (`/app/ongoing-today`) dan **Trip Command Center** (`/app/trip-command-center`) sebagai URL link open_in_new_tab 0 (ikut pola Trip Manager sedia ada); pindahkan **Travel Resources** (`/app/travel-resources`) ke seksyen ini dari SUMBER & KREW (open_in_new_tab 0).
- Bump `modified` timestamp.

### 2. Workspace — `travel_booking/travel_booking/module_travel_booking/workspace/travel_booking/travel_booking.json`
- **shortcuts array** (kini 1 → 4, halaman yang benar-benar wujud): Manage Booking (manage-booking-app, kekal), Trip Manager (trip-manager), Trip Command Center (trip-command-center), Ongoing Today (ongoing-today) — bentuk `{doc_view, label, link_to, type: "Page"}`.
- **content** (JSON string): gantikan 4 blok shortcut tergantung (sc_dashboard "Dashboard & Reports", sc_manage_trip, sc_booking_hub, sc_trip_cmd) dengan 4 shortcut sebenar di atas; **buang blok kad "Portal Links"** (Workspace Link tiada sokongan URL — portal URLs sudah diliputi sidebar); tambah blok kad baharu "B2B & Leads" (col 6, seimbang dengan Dashboard Pages).
- **links array**:
  - Isi kanak-kanak kad **"Dashboard Pages"** (Card Break sedia ada, link_count 4, kini kosong): 4 Page link (manage-booking-app, trip-manager, trip-command-center, ongoing-today).
  - Tambah link DocType: **Cruise Cabin** → kad Cruise Operations; **Booking Amendment** → kad Booking Operations; **Flight Schedule** → kad Flight Operations; **Travel Price Rule** → kad Vouchers & Promotions.
  - **Card Break baharu "B2B & Leads"** + link Travel B2B Partner, Travel Inquiry.
  - Kemas kini `link_count` setiap Card Break yang diubah (Cruise Operations 2→3, Booking Operations 3→4, Flight Operations 3→4, Vouchers & Promotions 2→3).
- **charts array**: `[{chart_name: "Traveller Enrollment", label: "Traveller Enrollment"}]` (chart JSON wujud di `module_travel_booking/dashboard_chart/traveller_enrollment/`).
- **number_cards array**: 4 baris `{number_card_name, label}` dengan nama yang SAMA dengan blok content sedia ada — "Pending (for Payment)", "Accepted (Ready to Process)", "Currently Processing", "Abandoned" (kurang churn; jika rekod lama wujud dalam DB, import mengemas kininya tanpa mencipta yatim).
- Bump `modified` timestamp.
