Privacy Notice
How we collect, use and protect your personal data.
1. Who we are

We operate the Rarecation travel booking portal ("the Portal", "we", "us"). Our head office is in Malaysia with a sales office in Singapore.

[REVIEW — isikan nama entiti berdaftar + nombor pendaftaran selepas pengesahan legal.]

2. What we collect
  • Account & contact — name, email address, phone number
  • Booking & travel — trip selections, cabin assignments, flight details
  • Traveller documents — IC number, passport number & expiry, passport copy image, visa photo, nationality, date of birth, gender
  • Emergency contact — name, phone, relationship (provided by you)
  • Health info you choose to share — dietary needs, medical conditions, special needs (optional)
  • Payment — transaction references and receipts (card details are processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers)
3. Why we collect it (purposes)
  • Processing and fulfilling your trip bookings
  • Cruise manifest, immigration and travel authority requirements — passport & travel document data is operationally required for these purposes
  • Verifying payments and issuing receipts and invoices
  • Communicating with you about your booking, itinerary changes and trip updates
  • Remembering your traveller details so you don't need to re-enter them for future trips (only with your consent)
  • Meeting legal, tax and accounting obligations
4. Who we share it with

We share your data only as necessary to operate your trip:

  • Cruise operators and transport providers (names, passport details for manifests)
  • Payment processor (Stripe) for card payments
  • Relevant authorities where required by law

[REVIEW — senaraikan sub-processor spesifik + asas pemindahan rentas sempadan (Singapura) selepas pengesahan legal.]

We do not sell your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Traveller and passport details are retained to serve your future bookings conveniently, and for the period needed to meet accounting, tax and legal obligations. You may request deletion at any time via your Profile page — passport images will be removed, while some financial records may be retained where the law requires.

[REVIEW — tetapkan tempoh retensi spesifik (cth. X tahun selepas tarikh trip) selepas pengesahan legal.]

6. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. remembering traveller details)

Submit any of these requests from your Profile page, or contact us directly — see Section 8.

7. Security

Passport copies and visa photos are stored as private files with restricted access. Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it to serve your booking. Card details are processed entirely by Stripe and never touch our servers.
